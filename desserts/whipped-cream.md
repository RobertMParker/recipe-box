# Whipped Cream

* 1 cup cold, heavy cream
* 2 tablespoons confectioners sugar
* 1 teaspoon vanilla

Instructions:
1. Combine ingredients in a mixing bowl
1. Use handmixer on medium to mix ingredients
1. Stop when you have soft peaks


Note:
I've read that adding 1/4 teaspoon of cream of tartar will help stabilize the whipped cream
