# Salsa Verde

Ingredients:
* 1.5 pounds tomatillos halved
* 1/2 onion quartered
* 2 cloves garlic
* 2 jalapeño peppers halved w/ seeds
* 2 serrano peppers halved w/ seeds
* Salt to taste
* 1/2 cup chopped cilantro leaves and stems
* juice from one lime

Steps:
1. Turn broiler on high
1. Put tomatillos, onion, garlic, and peppers on bare baking sheet skin side up. They should blacken on top.
1. Broil for ~7 minutes.  Keep an eye on things, and remove peppers early as needed.
1. Put all ingredients in food processor and blend
1. Put in fridge overnight, it should mellow out a bit.

Notes: This is a cross between [this recipe](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/tomatillo_salsa_verde/) and some techniques that I learned from Estefano Salgado
