# Carolina Mustard BBQ Sauce

Ingredients:
1. 1 cup yellow mustard
1. 1/2 cup granulated sugar
1. 1/3 cup light brown sugar
1. 3/4 cup cider vinegar
1. 1/4 cup water
1. 2 tablespoons mild chili powder 
1. 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
1. 1 teaspoon white pepper
1. 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
1. 1 teaspoon soy sauce
1. 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
1. 1 tablespoon liquid smoke, hickory flavor

Steps:
1. Combine mustard, sugar, brown sugar, cider vinegar, water, chili powder, pepper, white pepper, cayenne in a medium saucepan.
1. Simmer on low for 20 minutes, making sure to stir occasionally.
1. Add soy sauce, unsalted butter, and liquid smoke and simmer for 10 more minutes.

Notes: Freeze leftovers in icecube trays
